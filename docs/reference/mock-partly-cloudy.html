A cloud's size range, contrast, brightness, lightness and height belong to the section it's in, so the same cloud can look different in the front and the middle. This preview is the size of the real homepage: the box fills your screen and the sky is its top 40%, and everything scales the way the site does: sizes are set at a 1440×900 window, and wider or taller screens grow the clouds and add a few more to fill the width.
